1. Executive Summary

CONDITIONAL GO   Integration Complexity: Critical

Strategic Rationale. Acquiring Databricks cements Microsoft Azure as the undisputed enterprise AI & data control plane, unifying Databricks' industry-leading Lakehouse/Mosaic AI stack (~$3.0B+ ARR, >55% YoY growth) with Microsoft Fabric and Azure OpenAI workloads. The combined platform unlocks massive Azure cloud pull-through across Fortune 500 enterprises while neutralizing Snowflake and AWS Bedrock's primary data-gravity wedge. However, intense FTC/EU/CMA vertical-foreclosure scrutiny over AWS and GCP multi-cloud commitments makes binding multi-cloud neutrality remedies mandatory for closing.

Recommendation Stance. Proceed conditionally with a proactive 10-year legally binding Multi-Cloud Parity Commitment (mirroring the Activision/Call of Duty behavioral remedy playbook), an independent Databricks business unit inside Azure (LinkedIn/GitHub governance model), and unified Delta/Iceberg open-table interoperability with Microsoft Fabric.

Valuation Commentary. At $52.0B EV (~17x NTM ARR), valuation is rich on standalone multiples but highly compelling on an all-in platform basis: probability-weighted Synergy NPV of ~$15.9B reduces the effective acquisition multiple to ~12x NTM ARR for a >55% YoY growth asset with >140% net dollar retention. Accretive to non-GAAP EPS by Year 3.
